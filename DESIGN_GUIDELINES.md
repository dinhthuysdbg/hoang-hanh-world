# Hoàng Hạnh's World — Design Guidelines

## Product direction
A private, romantic digital keepsake for Hoàng Hạnh. The experience should feel personal, calm, editorial and carefully art-directed — never like a generic template.

## Visual system
- Primary: pastel rose; secondary: lavender; supporting accents: peach and baby blue.
- Backgrounds: white and barely-tinted blush. Avoid large saturated pink areas.
- Display type: Playfair Display. Interface/body: DM Sans. All copy must render Vietnamese diacritics correctly.
- Surfaces: fine borders, restrained shadows, moderate radii. Avoid oversized rounded cards.
- Motion: subtle 200–300ms transitions; respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Icons: simple consistent line/typographic marks; do not mix icon libraries.

## UX rules
- Mobile-first, no horizontal overflow, touch targets at least 40px.
- Clear hierarchy, compact navigation, readable line lengths.
- Every page must reuse shared tokens and components.
- Validate at 390px mobile and 1440px desktop before declaring complete.
- Preserve the design system unless explicitly approved to change it.

## Quality gate
Check layout, typography, color, icon consistency, spacing, card/button states, motion, responsive behavior, Vietnamese text, accessibility and console/build errors.
